# Dumbbell Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

## Information stored on your device

Dumbbell is a free workout tracker that works without an account. It stores your name, training preferences, workouts, exercise sets, repetitions, weights, workout dates and durations, body-weight entries, and any workout photos you choose to attach. Training records and photos are stored in the app's private device storage.

## Collection and use

Dumbbell uses this information to display your profile, log and resume workouts, calculate progress, and create exports or summary images you request. The app does not send your training records, photos or profile to a developer server. The Android app uses Google AdMob to display banner advertising on the History screen and a full-screen ad after every second successfully finished workout, when an ad is available. Workouts are saved before a full-screen ad appears. A local completion counter remembers this frequency across app restarts. Workout logging and stored exercise information remain available offline; ads and consent messages use an internet connection. We do not send your name, training records, body-weight entries or photos to AdMob for advertising.

## Advertising and privacy choices

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostic information such as performance and crash data. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Advertising may be personalized depending on your choices and applicable requirements. We do not add a separate analytics or automatic crash-reporting service.

Google's User Messaging Platform manages applicable consent messages before ad requests. Where available, use Settings → Ad privacy choices to review or change your choices. Device advertising controls are also available in Android Settings. Your ability to log workouts does not depend on agreeing to personalized advertising.

Google describes its processing at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Ad interactions and any advertiser websites you visit are handled under those services' policies.

## Photos, files and optional notifications

Camera and photo/file selection are initiated by you using your device's system tools. Dumbbell accesses only the images or backup files you select. Selected workout photos are copied into private app storage. The app does not continuously access your camera or photo library. You may enable workout notifications to see elapsed time and the current exercise. Notification details and visibility are controlled by your device settings. Declining notifications does not prevent workout logging.

## Sharing and external services

Dumbbell only creates exports and opens the system share sheet when you ask. A workout summary can include the workout's title, date, duration, exercises, completed-set totals and your attached photo if selected. It does not include your profile by default. Files you save or share may be accessible to your chosen destination, other apps or people. Those services handle the files under their own privacy policies. Exercise source links open an external browser, whose provider may receive normal web request information.

## Retention, backups and deletion

Records stay in the app until you delete them or remove its storage. You can delete workouts, individual sets, photos and body-weight entries using their controls. To remove all profile and training information, clear Dumbbell's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. Copies you exported or shared must be removed separately from their destinations.

Automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer of private app data are disabled. Export a Backup CSV regularly if you want a restorable copy. Backup CSV files exclude profile details and photos. A normal signed app update preserves private local records; uninstalling the app or losing the device can remove them. Dumbbell has no server copy to restore.

## Security

The app uses Android's private app storage and device protections. It does not add a separate database encryption password or app lock. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep exported backups private.

## Exercise information

Dumbbell provides general fitness logging and informational exercise illustrations. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Exercise illustrations are supplied by RepDB and are AI-generated. Use qualified guidance when deciding which exercises are appropriate for you.
